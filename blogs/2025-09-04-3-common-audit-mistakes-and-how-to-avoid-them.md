In highly regulated industries, audits are or should not be just a compliance checkbox — they are a cornerstone of ensuring patient safety and supply chain resilience. Yet, many organizations struggle to get the full value from their audits. When mismanaged, audits can drain resources, delay projects, and completely miss their function.
